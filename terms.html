2. What the App does

A field tool for agronomic work: grower, farm and field records with GPS boundaries; automatic sampling grids and GPS navigation to each point; sample barcode scanning; offline work with later sync; fertility reports and maps; field notebook (applications, pests, weeds, diary, rainfall, photo timeline); satellite imagery, NDVI and weather; exports in PDF, KML, GeoJSON, CSV and Excel. Business modules (inventory, grain storage, invoices, finance) are restricted to authorized staff.
